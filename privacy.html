Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Operated by Taehyun Joo

The short version. You sign in with Google or Apple, choose a nickname, and say whether you are a KATUSA or with USFK. We keep what you post so others can see it. We never ask for or show your unit, rank, base, or family. We don’t use your location, we don’t run ads or analytics, and we don’t sell anything about you. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

1. What we collect and why

WhatWhy

Some things stay only on your phone: a copy of your profile, whether you’ve agreed to the community rules, and which answers you’ve already seen. Signing out or deleting the app removes them.

2. What other members see

  • Your nickname and what you post.
  • KATUSA or USFK only next to restaurant recommendations you write and on your coffee chat profile, if you make one. It is hidden in the market.
  • Your Signal link, to signed-in members, on your listings so buyers can message you.
  • A club’s Signal group link only to members the organizer approved.
  • The contact in a coffee chat request only to the person you sent it to, after they accept.
  • Never your email, your Google or Apple name, or your photo there. Club member lists are not public.

3. How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything you posted: until you delete your account. Deletion removes them right away.
  • Expired market listings: deleted 90 days after they expire (sold listings, about 270 days after they’re marked sold).
  • Bans: if an account is banned, we keep the sign-in provider and account ID (not the email) after deletion, so the same account can’t sign up again. We keep it while the ban lasts.
  • Moderation records: a log of what we hid or removed and why. It refers to posts by ID and doesn’t keep the post itself. We keep it to show how we handled reports.
  • Server logs: a short time, set by our hosting provider.

When the time is up we delete the data from the database and storage. There are no paper records.

4. Sharing

We don’t sell or give your information to anyone. We share it only when the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order.

5. Your choices and rights

  • See and fix your information: My profile › Edit profile, or edit your posts.
  • Delete your account and everything you posted: My profile › Delete account. If you can’t open the app, see how to ask us to delete it.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, to stop processing it, or anything else about your data at jth3585@gmail.com. We reply within 10 days.

If you’re in Korea, you can also contact the Personal Information Protection Commission (privacy.go.kr) or the KISA privacy center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, call 118).

6. How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database rules decide row by row who can read and change what. You can only change your own posts.
  • Only the operator can use admin tools, and every moderation action is logged.

7. Age

Chingu is for people 18 and over.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
