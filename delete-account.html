Delete your Chingu account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Chingu and tap your initial in the top right to open My profile.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more.

Your account is deleted right away.

Can’t open the app?

Email jth3585@gmail.com from the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with, and include your Chingu nickname. We’ll delete the account within 10 days and reply when it’s done.

What’s deleted

  • Your profile: nickname, KATUSA or USFK, Signal link
  • Your questions and answers, market listings and their photos, restaurant recommendations, and place suggestions
  • Your coffee chat profile and the coffee chat requests you sent or received
  • Clubs you run on your own, your club memberships, your saved listings, the people you blocked, and the reports you sent
  • Your sign-in record, including your email address

If you run a club that has other members, hand it off to your co-organizer or close it first. The app tells you if this applies.

What’s kept

  • If your account was banned: your sign-in provider and account ID (not your email), so the same account can’t sign up again.
  • Our moderation log, which records actions on reported posts by ID, not the posts themselves.

Messages you exchanged on Signal are outside Chingu and are not affected.
